# Risk run loot until leaving a checkpoint

Each generated floor contains a starting room, combat rooms, and a boss. A
successful clear awards run loot and experience, adjusts the permanent account
Delve Level, and offers a choice to leave safely or continue with remaining
health. Run loot is banked only on leaving safely at a checkpoint; death loses
all loot acquired during the active run while retaining earned experience.

This supersedes banking items at every floor clear. Putting the entire run's
loot at risk gives continuing a meaningful cost, balanced by more item drops,
higher quality chances, extra experience, and larger Delve Level gains from
successful consecutive floors. Improving rare-item chance is the primary
continuation incentive. The difficulty bonus acts through the permanent
Delve Level; a separate temporary encounter difficulty bonus was not selected.

Experience improves character stats. Performance scoring combines outcome,
speed, damage avoidance, and accuracy, initially providing at most 10 percent
greater gains. Exact measurements and weights remain to be designed. Losses
become more substantial later in progression. Run loot can be equipped at
checkpoints while remaining at risk, and unfinished runs can resume across
sessions.

Deliberate mid-floor abandonment has the same consequences as death; closing
the app pauses the run. A fresh run restores health and shields and resets
depth and continuation bonuses.
